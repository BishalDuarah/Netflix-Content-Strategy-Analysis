# 🎬 Netflix Content Strategy Analysis using CRISP-DM

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Netflix's content catalog using the **CRISP-DM (Cross Industry Standard Process for Data Mining)** framework to identify business opportunities, content gaps, and strategic recommendations.

The objective is to simulate the role of a Data Analyst at Netflix by converting raw data into actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Netflix invests heavily in acquiring and producing content. Understanding how content is distributed across countries, genres, ratings, and release years can help optimize future investments and improve subscriber engagement.

This project answers key business questions such as:

* Which countries contribute the most content?
* What genres dominate Netflix?
* Are Movies or TV Shows more prevalent?
* How has Netflix's content library evolved over time?
* Which audience ratings are most common?
* What business opportunities can be identified from the data?

---

## 📊 Dataset

* **Source:** Kaggle
* **Dataset:** Netflix Movies and TV Shows
* **Records:** ~8,800 titles
* **Format:** CSV

---

## 🧠 CRISP-DM Framework

### 1. Business Understanding

* Defined business objectives
* Identified key analytical questions
* Established project success criteria

### 2. Data Understanding

* Explored dataset structure
* Identified missing values
* Performed data quality assessment

### 3. Data Preparation

* Removed duplicate records
* Handled missing values
* Converted date formats
* Engineered new features (Content Age, Decade, Primary Country, Primary Genre)

### 4. Exploratory Data Analysis

Generated visualizations to analyze:

* Content Type Distribution
* Top Producing Countries
* Genre Distribution
* Content Growth Over Time
* Ratings Distribution
* Movie Duration
* Top Directors
* Monthly Content Additions
* Content Age

### 5. Evaluation

Business findings were evaluated against the original objectives and translated into strategic recommendations.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Movies dominate Netflix's catalog.
* Content production is concentrated in a few countries.
* Drama and International Movies are the leading genres.
* Most content targets adult audiences.
* Netflix experienced rapid content growth after 2015.
* Educational and Science content remain underrepresented.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Increase investment in TV Shows to improve subscriber retention.
* Expand production in underrepresented regions.
* Diversify the content portfolio with Educational and Science genres.
* Strengthen family-friendly programming.
* Collaborate with emerging directors to increase creative diversity.
